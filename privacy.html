Privacy & Cookies

HarderBPM respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you use HarderBPM, why it is collected and how you can control your cookie preferences.

1. About HarderBPM

HarderBPM is an online radio platform that makes it easy to discover and listen to radio stations focused on harder styles of electronic music.

HarderBPM does not require you to create an account in order to listen to the available radio stations.

2. Analytics

HarderBPM uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. This helps us understand things such as which pages are visited, how the website is accessed and how the service can be improved.

Analytics is only enabled after you give permission for analytical cookies through the cookie banner.

3. Cookies and consent

When you first visit HarderBPM, you can choose whether to allow analytical cookies.

If you choose “Necessary only”, analytical cookie storage remains disabled. If you choose “Accept”, analytical storage for Google Analytics is enabled.

HarderBPM stores your cookie preference in your browser so that you do not have to make the same choice every time you visit the website.

4. Google Analytics

Google Analytics is provided by Google. When analytics consent has been granted, Google may process information about the use of HarderBPM in accordance with its own privacy policies.

HarderBPM does not use Google Analytics for personalised advertising.

You can learn more about how Google handles information on Google's privacy website.

5. Radio streams

HarderBPM provides access to radio streams operated by third-party radio stations.

When you choose to play a station, your browser connects to the streaming infrastructure of that station or its streaming provider. Those third parties may process technical information such as your IP address according to their own privacy policies.

HarderBPM does not control the privacy practices of these third-party streaming services.

6. Technical information

Like most websites, hosting and infrastructure providers used by HarderBPM may process technical information necessary to securely deliver the website, such as IP addresses, request information and security logs.

7. Your choices

You can refuse analytical cookies and continue using the core functionality of HarderBPM.

You can also remove stored website data or cookie preferences through your browser settings. After doing so, HarderBPM may ask for your cookie preference again on your next visit.

8. Changes to this policy

This privacy and cookie policy may be updated when HarderBPM changes its services or the technologies used on the website.

The latest version will always be available on this page.
